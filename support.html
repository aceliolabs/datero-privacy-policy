Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a countdown?
Open Datero and create a countdown by entering an event name and date.

How do I use widgets?
Add a Datero widget to your iPhone Home Screen using the iOS widget gallery.

Why am I not receiving notifications?
Check that notifications are enabled for Datero in your iPhone Settings.
