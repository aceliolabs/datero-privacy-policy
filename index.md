# Privacy Policy for Datero

**Last updated: October 15, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

Datero ("the App") is a countdown application that allows users to create countdowns for birthdays, special occasions, and other events. The App includes home screen widgets and local notifications to help users keep track of upcoming events.

This Privacy Policy explains how Datero handles information when you use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information You Enter

Datero allows you to create and customise countdowns by entering information such as event titles, dates, and other event details.

This information is stored locally on your device and is used to display countdowns, update widgets, and schedule local notifications. Datero does not transmit this event information to our servers.

### Analytics Information

Datero uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the App is used and to improve its functionality and user experience.

Depending on the SDK configuration, this service may collect information such as:

- App interactions and usage events.
- App version and device information.
- Operating system information.
- App instance identifiers and related technical identifiers.
- General diagnostic and performance information.

### Crash Reports

Datero uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify, investigate, and fix app crashes and stability issues.

Crash reports may include crash stack traces, device and operating system information, app state information, and technical identifiers necessary to diagnose problems.

We do not intentionally include event titles or other personal information in analytics events or crash reports.

## 3. Local Notifications and Widgets

Datero uses Apple's local notification functionality to remind you of upcoming events.

Countdown widgets display information from the countdowns you create. These features operate using information available on your device.

Notifications and widgets do not require Datero to send your event details to a remote server. You can manage notification permissions through your device's settings.

## 4. Third-Party Services

Datero uses the following third-party services:

**Google Analytics for Firebase**

Used to understand app usage and improve the App.

**Firebase Crashlytics**

Used to detect, analyse, and resolve crashes and other stability issues.

These services are provided by Google LLC and its affiliates. Information processed by these services is subject to Google's applicable privacy policies and service terms.

For further information, please consult Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and Firebase's privacy information at https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

## 5. How We Use Information

Information processed through Datero and its third-party services is used to:

- Provide countdowns, widgets, and local notifications.
- Understand how the App is used.
- Identify and fix crashes and technical issues.
- Improve app performance, reliability, and functionality.

We do not sell your personal information or use it to deliver personalised advertising.

## 6. Data Storage and Retention

Countdown information is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain a remote database of your countdowns.

You can remove countdowns within the App, where supported, or remove locally stored app data by deleting the App. The availability and retention of data may depend on your device settings and backups.

Analytics and crash information may be retained by Google according to the applicable Firebase service settings and retention policies.

## 7. Data Sharing

Datero does not sell your personal information.

Technical and usage information may be processed by Google through Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for the purposes described in this policy. Such processing is subject to the applicable service terms, privacy policies, and legal requirements.

We do not otherwise share countdown information with third parties.

## 8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Datero. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Because countdown information is stored locally, its protection also depends on the security of your device and its backups.

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data.

You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise applicable rights or ask questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us using the details below.

## 10. Children's Privacy

Datero is a general-purpose countdown application and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information for our own purposes. The analytics and crash-reporting services described above may process technical information as explained in this policy.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Datero, our data practices, or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will be published at the URL provided for this policy.
